## Decision

Rule, fixed before the results were seen: IMERG has a step change if the Pettitt test gives p < 0.05 for the annual or the Jun-Sep totals of any district.

- **IMERG shows no significant change point** in any district, annual or Jun-Sep. IMERG is the default rain source (`load_weather(district)` in `src/compute/weather.py`) for the water balance, the hindcast and everything downstream.
- POWER: 10 of 10 series have p < 0.05 (Cumilla annual; Cumilla Jun-Sep; Feni annual; Feni Jun-Sep; Noakhali annual; Noakhali Jun-Sep; Brahmanbaria annual; Brahmanbaria Jun-Sep; Sylhet annual; Sylhet Jun-Sep).

## Do Feni and Noakhali now differ?

- POWER: identical rain on 100% of days (one shared cell).
- IMERG: different cells (23.05, 91.35 vs 22.85, 91.05); identical rain on 45% of days (mostly dry days on both), daily r = 0.85.
- IMERG mean annual total 2001-2024: Feni 2549 mm, Noakhali 2587 mm (Noakhali - Feni +38 mm; Noakhali wetter in 19 of 24 years; Wilcoxon signed-rank p=0.034).
- IMERG mean Jun-Sep total 2001-2025: Feni 1801 mm, Noakhali 1869 mm (Noakhali - Feni +68 mm; Noakhali wetter in 22 of 25 years; Wilcoxon p<0.001).
- **Yes, but only a little: with IMERG the two districts have their own rain records, and Noakhali is consistently wetter, by about 1% a year (4% in Jun-Sep). The neighbouring 0.1 degree cells share their monthly gauge adjustment, so treat this as a small difference, not a headline contrast.** Temperature, humidity, wind and solar radiation still come from the one shared POWER cell, so any contrast is in rain only.
